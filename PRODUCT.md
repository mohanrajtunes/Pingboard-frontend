# PingBoard — product truth

PingBoard is an uptime monitor. It checks public URLs every 1–60 minutes, waits for a second failed check before opening an incident, and sends one email when a site breaks and one when it recovers. Users get a dashboard (monitors, uptime, response time, incidents) and an optional public status page.

Audience: developers and small teams who want to know the minute a site goes down without noise.
Surface in scope: the landing page and login/register (Persuade). The app screens stay as they are for now.
Brief from the owner: Marshmallow White (#F0F0F0), Galactic Violet, Bubblegum Pink; light theme; huge uppercase staggered hero heading; glass cards; liquid-metal pulse mark; smooth motion.
Meaning of colour: violet is the brand; pink only ever means "something is wrong".
Content rules: no invented customers, quotes or statistics. All sample monitors are example.dev data.
